How CTICD Correlation Works

CTICD synthesizes multi-source intelligence to reconstruct the adversary's attack campaign. Submitting any indicator automatically discovers shared C2 infrastructure, attributes the operational threat group, maps the technique to the MITRE ATT&CK Matrix, links weaponized CVE vulnerabilities, and constructs a Diamond Model profile.

1. Ingestion

Deconstructs input IOC, computes cryptographic seeds, and queries the local CTI cache.

2. Attribution

Correlates operational infrastructure with state-sponsored APTs and ransomware groups.

3. Graph Linking

Builds semantic relationships between malware, C2 IPs, domains, hashes, and targeted sectors.

4. Countermeasures

Auto-generates defensive YARA signatures, Snort IDS rules, and Sigma SIEM detection queries.
